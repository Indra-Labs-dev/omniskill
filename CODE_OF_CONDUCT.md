# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want OMNISKILL to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, language, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith; disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive, evidence-based feedback.
- Be honest about results, including failures and limits — this is the spirit of the project.
- Help newcomers, in French or English.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Deliberately misleading results, fabricated benchmarks or fake reviews.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour breaks this code. Reports are handled confidentially.

Report incidents privately to the maintainers through the contact listed in `SECURITY.md` (use the subject "Code of Conduct").
